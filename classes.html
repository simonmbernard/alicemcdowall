REFORMER PILATES

Small group Reformer classes of five, focused on length, strength and tone. A total body workout, every time.

01 Beginners−

Learn the core repertoire and FRAME method at a slower pace, alongside other newcomers. For those new to the Tempo Pilates method or Pilates in general; suitable for all levels.

02 All Levels+

A flowing, full-body class using props and intermediate exercises to lengthen, strengthen and tone. Basic Reformer knowledge is recommended. Attend at least one Beginner class first.

03 Intermediate+

A dynamic full-body workout with weights and props to challenge you and make you sweat. We recommend at least five Beginner or All Levels classes first.

04 HIIT Reformer+

High energy, a faster pace and heavier loads, including weights, to build strength and stamina. We recommend at least three Beginner classes first.

05 Private 1–1+

Personal sessions for any age and fitness level. For those new to Pilates, recovering from injury, working towards athletic goals or wanting more from their practice. Contact us to discuss your needs and availability.
