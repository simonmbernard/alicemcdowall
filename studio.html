Founded on the Wirral in 2023, FRAME is a boutique Pilates studio specialising in private and group sessions. Our peaceful, intimate setting creates space for focus and connection to mind, body and soul.

We bring modern equipment together with time-honoured Pilates principles. Through slow, controlled movement, precise form and thoughtful breathing, each session builds strength, flexibility and inner balance.

More about our philosophy +

Our focus is form and precision: targeting small muscle groups, lengthening muscles and paying close attention to technique and posture. Sessions are tailored to individual needs while challenging endurance and strength. Our equipment supports maximum functionality and innovative exercises, with physical and mental well-being at the heart of our practice.
