A LITTLE MORE TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

New to Reformer Pilates? +

Start with a Beginners class. You’ll learn the equipment and core movements at a slower pace, with a focus on form, breathing and the FRAME method.

Which class should I choose? +

Beginners is the starting point. Attend at least one Beginner class before All Levels, five Beginner or All Levels classes before Intermediate, and three Beginner classes before HIIT Reformer.

Can I book a private session? +

Yes. Our private 1–1 sessions are tailored to your needs and goals. Email info@framepilatesstudio.com to discuss availability.

Where can I find FRAME? +

Our four studios are in Moreton, Bromborough, Chester and Oxton. Choose your studio below to find directions and book.

What if I need to cancel? +

Cancel or reschedule through your account at least 48 hours before your Reformer class. Cancellations within 48 hours incur the full session fee.
