01 / THE FRAME APPROACH

Small movements.
Profound change.

A space to reconnect.
A practice to make your own.

Founded on the Wirral in 2023, FRAME is a boutique Pilates studio specialising in private and group sessions. Our peaceful, intimate setting creates space for focus and connection to mind, body and soul.

We bring modern equipment together with time-honoured Pilates principles. Through slow, controlled movement, precise form and thoughtful breathing, each session builds strength, flexibility and inner balance.

More about our philosophy +

Our focus is form and precision: targeting small muscle groups, lengthening muscles and paying close attention to technique and posture. Sessions are tailored to individual needs while challenging endurance and strength. Our equipment supports maximum functionality and innovative exercises, with physical and mental well-being at the heart of our practice.

LENGTH. STRENGTH. CONNECTION.
REFORMER PILATES

Small group Reformer classes of five, focused on length, strength and tone. A total body workout, every time.

01 Beginners−

Learn the core repertoire and FRAME method at a slower pace, alongside other newcomers. For those new to the Tempo Pilates method or Pilates in general; suitable for all levels.

02 All Levels+

A flowing, full-body class using props and intermediate exercises to lengthen, strengthen and tone. Basic Reformer knowledge is recommended. Attend at least one Beginner class first.

03 Intermediate+

A dynamic full-body workout with weights and props to challenge you and make you sweat. We recommend at least five Beginner or All Levels classes first.

04 HIIT Reformer+

High energy, a faster pace and heavier loads, including weights, to build strength and stamina. We recommend at least three Beginner classes first.

05 Private 1–1+

Personal sessions for any age and fitness level. For those new to Pilates, recovering from injury, working towards athletic goals or wanting more from their practice. Contact us to discuss your needs and availability.

A LITTLE MORE TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

New to Reformer Pilates? +

Start with a Beginners class. You’ll learn the equipment and core movements at a slower pace, with a focus on form, breathing and the FRAME method.

Which class should I choose? +

Beginners is the starting point. Attend at least one Beginner class before All Levels, five Beginner or All Levels classes before Intermediate, and three Beginner classes before HIIT Reformer.

Can I book a private session? +

Yes. Our private 1–1 sessions are tailored to your needs and goals. Email info@framepilatesstudio.com to discuss availability.

Where can I find FRAME? +

Our four studios are in Moreton, Bromborough, Chester and Oxton. Choose your studio below to find directions and book.

What if I need to cancel? +

Cancel or reschedule through your account at least 48 hours before your Reformer class. Cancellations within 48 hours incur the full session fee.
